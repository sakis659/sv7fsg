Summary

AJ6VJ will be QRV as KH0/AJ6VJ from Saipan in the Northern Mariana Islands, 29 December to 3 January, on 40–10m (maybe 80m) using CW and FT8. It is a tough path from Europe, so try the higher HF bands in the morning.
